HieroOCR reads a page of printed Egyptian hieroglyphic text —a camera photo, an image or a PDF— and transcribes it to Manuel de Codage (MdC), ready to paste into JSesh or any other editor.

• Opens images (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC…) and PDFs from your phone, SD card or Drive. For multi-page PDFs you choose which pages to recognize (7, 71-92, 3,5,7-9…).
• Take a photo of the page with the camera: it is straightened and its lighting corrected before recognition.
• Every sign is outlined with its Gardiner code and colored by recognition confidence. Tap a sign to see its five most likely candidates with their phonetic values, and fix it with one tap.
• HieroOCR learns from your corrections: further pages of the same book are recognized better.
• Built-in OpenGlyph dictionary and corpus: search for a sign or a group of signs, see which word each sign belongs to and the reading of every line. The corpus also helps to choose between similar signs.
• Share the MdC of the whole document by email, Drive, Telegram… or copy it to the clipboard.
• Works offline. No ads, no account and no data collection: everything is processed on your device.

Designed for hieroglyphic text printed in horizontal lines (grammars, text editions, dictionaries). Carved or painted inscriptions and columns are recognized less reliably. The interface is in Spanish.

HieroOCR uses SDL2, the OpenGlyph dictionary and corpus, and the Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs font.
